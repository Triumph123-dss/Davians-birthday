A Letter From Mum

Words from a mother's heart

Happy 2nd Birthday,
My Precious Davian ❤️

My darling Davian,

Two years ago, God blessed my life with something more precious than words could ever explain — you.

From the very first moment I held you, my heart knew that my life would never be the same again.

Watching you grow, hearing your laughter, seeing your little smiles and watching you discover the world have given me memories that I will treasure forever.

My son, you are a beautiful blessing, a source of joy and a precious reminder of God's love.

As you grow older, I want you to always remember one thing: Mummy loves you.

No matter how tall you become, no matter how far life takes you, you will always be my baby boy.

🙏 Mummy's Prayer For You

May God Almighty protect you every day of your life.

May He guide your footsteps, give you wisdom, surround you with good people and keep your heart filled with kindness and love.

May you grow strong, healthy, intelligent and courageous.

May God open beautiful doors for you and lead you into a future greater than anything we could ever imagine.

May His hand remain upon you all the days of your life.

In Jesus' name, Amen. ❤️

Happy 2nd Birthday, my precious son. 🎂

Mummy loves you more than words could ever explain.

❤️
— Mum
