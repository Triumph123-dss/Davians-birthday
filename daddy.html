A Letter From Daddy

A few words from a father's heart

🤍

Happy 2nd Birthday,
My Son Davian

My precious Davian,

Two years ago, you came into my life and gave my heart a love I can never put into words.

Watching you grow has been one of the greatest blessings of my life. Every smile, every little laugh, every new thing you learn reminds me just how precious you are.

You may be little today, but you are already a big part of my heart. I pray that God protects you, guides you, and makes you a great man filled with wisdom, strength, kindness and purpose.

My son, I want you to always remember that you are loved. No matter how much you grow, no matter where life takes you, you will always have a special place in my heart.

Daddy will believe in you, support you and stand by you every step of the way.

🙏 Daddy's Prayer For You

May God Almighty watch over you and keep you safe wherever you go.

May He give you wisdom beyond your years, strength when life becomes difficult, kindness toward others and courage to follow what is right.

May your future be filled with purpose, favor, good health, peace and happiness. May you grow into a man who makes your family proud and whose life becomes a blessing to many.

May God's hand remain upon you all the days of your life. Amen. ❤️

Happy 2nd Birthday,
my precious son. ❤️

Daddy loves you endlessly.
♡
— Daddy
